Our story

A haveli turned
into a home.

Gour Hotels began in 1998 when the Gour family opened the doors of their Civil Lines residence to travellers exploring the Pink City. Three decades on, we're a small, proudly Indian hotel group known for genuine warmth over grand gestures.

Today our Jaipur flagship sits alongside sister properties in Udaipur and Jodhpur, but every Gour Hotel keeps the same promise — comfortable rooms, honest food, and people who treat you like family.

About Rasoi

Home-style food,
plated properly.

Rasoi is our all-day, multi-cuisine restaurant — Rajasthani thalis, familiar Indian favourites, and a short global menu for guests craving something else.

In the evenings, head up to Chai Baari, our rooftop café, for chai, snacks and views over Jaipur's rooftops as the sun goes down.

A few house favourites

Dal Baati Churma, Laal Maas and Ghewar are the three dishes guests ask us to recreate at home the most — our chefs won't share the recipes, but they will happily serve seconds.

Breakfast is complimentary for all room guests; à la carte pricing below applies to walk-in and non-resident diners.

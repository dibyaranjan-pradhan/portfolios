Cancellation Policy

  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your scheduled check-in time.
  • Cancellations made within 24 hours of check-in, or no-shows, are charged the cost of the first night's stay.
  • Bookings made under non-refundable or promotional rates are not eligible for cancellation or refund.
  • Group bookings (5+ rooms) and bookings during peak season (Oct–Mar) may carry a stricter cancellation window, communicated at the time of booking.
  • To cancel, contact us at stay@gourhotels.in or call +91 141 456 7890 quoting your booking reference.

Booking Policy

  • Reservations can be made through this website, by phone, by email, or via our listed travel partner platforms.
  • A valid government-issued photo ID is required from all guests at check-in, per local regulations.
  • Check-in is from 12:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM; early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability.
  • Room rates are quoted per night, inclusive of applicable taxes, and are confirmed only once a booking reference is issued.
  • Rates and availability may change until a booking is confirmed; confirmed bookings honour the rate shown at the time of confirmation.

Refund Policy

  • Where a cancellation is eligible for a refund under our Cancellation Policy, refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.
  • Refunds for bookings made through third-party travel platforms are subject to that platform's own refund timelines and process.
  • In the rare event we are unable to honour a confirmed booking, guests will be offered a full refund or an upgrade at a comparable Gour Hotels property, at the guest's choice.
  • No refunds are issued for early departures or unused nights once a stay has commenced, except where required by law.

Privacy Policy

  • We collect only the guest information needed to process reservations, verify identity at check-in, and communicate about your stay — name, contact details, government ID and payment information.
  • Guest information is never sold. It is shared only with staff who need it to serve you, and with payment processors or authorities where legally required.
  • Information submitted through our booking and contact forms is used solely to respond to that enquiry or reservation.
  • You may request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of your personal data by writing to stay@gourhotels.in.
  • Our website uses only essential cookies required for the site and booking form to function correctly.

Terms & Conditions

  • By booking a stay with Gour Hotels, guests agree to these policies along with any rate-specific terms shown at the time of booking.
  • Guests are responsible for any damage caused to hotel property during their stay, beyond normal wear and tear.
  • Smoking is permitted only in designated outdoor areas; a cleaning charge applies for smoking inside rooms.
  • The hotel reserves the right to refuse or discontinue service to any guest behaving in a manner that endangers staff, other guests, or property.
  • Content on this website — including photography, room descriptions and pricing — is for information only and may be updated without notice.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of India, with courts in Jaipur, Rajasthan having jurisdiction over any dispute.

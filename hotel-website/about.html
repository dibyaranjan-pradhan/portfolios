Our history

It started with
one guest room.

In 1998, Mahendra Gour converted a wing of his family's C-Scheme residence into a five-room guest house for travellers visiting Jaipur. Word spread quickly — mostly because guests kept coming back.

Nearly three decades later, that guest house has grown into the 48-room Gour Hotels Jaipur, with sister properties now open in Udaipur and Jodhpur. The building has changed — we've added a pool, a restaurant and a banquet hall — but it's still run day-to-day by the same family, and many of our staff have been with us for over a decade.
